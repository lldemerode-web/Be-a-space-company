How to Play

You're in charge of NASA. Each turn is one year. Keep public happiness high, manage funding, hire specialists, and complete six launches.

  1. Funding: At the start of each year, collect your annual funding. Every 0.5% of the # funding track is worth 1 MD per year.
  2. Card: Optionally draw one card, then optionally play up to one card from your hand. Cards have costs and effects.
  3. Action: Choose exactly one: start a launch, gain 3 MD, draw two cards, or buy any affordable quantity of fuel.
  4. Time: Each mission takes the years listed on its launch card. Active missions advance when you finish a year. Rewards and +1 successful launch arrive when the countdown ends.
  5. Employees: Recruitment cards hire specialists. Media Relations can waive the cost of Sensitive Talk; Expert can waive Explain About Space; Presidential Relations boosts Presidential Call; Astronauts can reduce some launch costs; Engineers enable inspections.
  6. Winning: Finish six successful launches and reach 90–100% happiness. Happiness at 0–10% ends the game in defeat.

Units: MD = million dollars; MG = million gallons; FP = MD per MG. Happiness changes in steps of 5%. Your progress saves automatically on this device.
